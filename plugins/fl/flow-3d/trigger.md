# Flow3D Studio Trigger & Routing

## Purpose
Flow3D Studio is the sovereign 3D procedural design and CAD engineering workspace inside Flowork OS. It provides real-time WebGL rendering, 34,840 local procedural 3D models across 7 modalities, AI prompt-to-3D parametric generation, and high-fidelity 360° YouTube/Shorts turntable video rendering.

## Activation Criteria
Activate this plugin when the user requests:
- Browsing or viewing procedural 3D models, CAD assemblies, or Three.js WebGL scenes.
- Converting or exporting OpenSCAD, CadQuery, or Three.js models to STL, STEP, GLB, or standalone HTML.
- Generating 3D objects or parametric CAD parts from natural language prompts.
- Creating 360° turntable animations or vertical video clips (9:16 Shorts / 16:9 Landscape) of 3D objects for YouTube content.
