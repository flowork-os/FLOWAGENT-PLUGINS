CRUSADER'S FIELD GUIDE

BE THEIR SHIELD.

Escort the rescue convoy across Eichenwalde's bridge. Stay inside its golden ring to advance. Clear enemies crowding the wagon, and keep yourself and the convoy alive through three escalating waves.

ROCKET HAMMER CLICK

A broad, close-range cleave. Drop your barrier to swing. Hold to keep striking.

BARRIER FIELD SPACE / RMB

Hold to intercept shots in front of you. Stand between threats and the convoy. Lower it to recharge. Melee attacks pass through.

FIRE STRIKE E

Launch a piercing wave of fire. Two charges; one returns every five seconds. Line up your enemies.

CHARGE SHIFT

Commit to a rocket-powered rush. Pin anything in your path. A long charge can leave the convoy exposed.

EARTHSHATTER Q

Earn it by dealing and blocking damage. Slam the ground to devastate and stun enemies ahead of you.

STAND WITH PURPOSE

WASD / arrows: move on screen. Mouse: aim. On touch, use the arrows and automatic aim. Esc: pause. R: restart. Each defeat repairs the convoy a little; quick defeats build an honor streak.
