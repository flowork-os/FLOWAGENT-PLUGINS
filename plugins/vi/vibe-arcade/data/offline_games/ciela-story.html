Tips

  • Walk into ropes, cords, ribbons and hanging sheets to climb them.
  • Run at a ledge to pull yourself up. Low blocks are hopped onto automatically.
  • Follow the golden star to find your next goal.
  • When you hear "Andy's coming!", freeze (F) or hide under the bed or desk before the door opens!
  • Jump on Andy's bed. Trust us.

On a phone or tablet: use the joystick to move, drag the right side to look, and tap the big buttons.
