About Squish Club


          
What is Squish Club?

          
A free browser game by Marco Mori (@macomoroni): a squishy, photoreal 3D head you can poke, pull, kiss, squeeze, punch, slap and pop. Every tool is free. Melt it, age it, record a clip and share it. No download, no account needed.


          
How to play

          
Pick a tool from the bar at the bottom, then touch or click the head. Drag to turn it, pinch to zoom. Hit Reset to put it back together.


          
Tools

          
All free: Pull, Poke, Kiss, Squeeze, Punch, Slap, Pimple, Pump, Paintball, Eggs, Rubber Bands and Suction; the Wobble, Clay and Jelly materials; the Age, Melt and Freeze effects; instant Replay and Slow-mo for your clips. On the Mullet, Bald, Grandpa and JP heads.


          
The Pimple tool

          
Squeeze a pimple until it pops. Gross, perfect, and free like every other tool.


          
Your Squish Twin

          
Upload a photo of yourself, or of an adult who has clearly agreed, and Squish Club builds your Squish Twin, a custom 3D head, from it: €2.99, one time. It is the only paid extra; every tool is already free.


          
Record & share a clip

          
Record up to 10 seconds, vertical. Slow down the moment you pick, then share it. All free.
