    How to play

    Free-for-all on huge destructible maps. First to 40 kills — or the best score after 6 minutes — wins the round.

    40kills wins the round
    6:00round time
    18pilots a server - bots fill every empty place
    8maps, a new one every round
    💥Everything breaks

    Buildings, towers, bridges, rides, trees and lamps can all be blown apart — cut the supports and it comes down on whoever is below.

    🗺Map vote

    A new map every round. Online, everyone votes at the end of a round: the next in the order or one of two others. Nobody votes? The next in the order.

    🟢Jump pads

    Green pads launch you onto rooftops, towers and cliffs — the fastest way up (and out of trouble).

    ➕Pickups

    Green crosses heal 50 HP. Purple Overcharge doubles your damage for 15 s.

    ✈Airstrike

    Grab the orange bomb on a tower roof (or earn it with a kill streak), aim and press G.

    ☢Nuke

    5 kills in a row without dying earns a NUKE. Aim and press N: 4 s of sirens, then it flattens everything within ~55 m.

    🤸Knockback

    Hits push enemies back — the lower their health, the further they fly. Knock them off roofs and cliffs.

    🏆Your stats

    All-time kills and deaths go on the leaderboard with your country flag; levels, medals and cosmetics are saved with your key.

    50 pilots in one dark world of solid rock, 350 by 350 metres. You start in a steel base of your own and dig your way to the others. First to 50 kills - or the most kills after 10 minutes - wins. Play it from the TUNNELER tile, the SERVERS list, or alone in PRACTICE.

    ⛏Digging
    • 1 the drill: hold fire and walk into the rock - it cuts a tunnel 4 m wide.
    • Tunnels only go sideways: the floor and the ceiling stay.
    • Rockets and dynamite blow craters in the rock too.
    • The drill hurts whoever stands right in front of it.
    💡The dark
    • Your gun has a light. 5 hangs a lamp on the wall - as many as you like.
    • Everybody sees a lamp's light - it can give you away.
    • Big caverns and old passages are hidden in the rock.
    👂Listen
    • You hear a drill only close up: a whisper at 15 m, loud right next to you. The DRILLING · m arrow shows drilling far off.
    • The bots hear you only 5 m away - and run when two or more come at them.
    🗺The map M
    • Top left: what you have seen. M or a tap opens the big one.
    • Your tunnels, somebody else's in orange (you are told when you break into one), old caves, the bases.
    • The drilling machines in yellow - an arrow on the small map points to the nearest.
    🔋Energy and your base
    • Drilling, moving and shooting use energy. Empty: no drill, no guns, half speed.
    • Only your base fills it - and your health (nothing heals outside). The BASE · m arrow shows the way home.
    • In somebody else's base energy fills at a third, health not at all.
    • You always come back to life in your base.
    🚜Drilling machines
    • Six stand in the big caverns. E climbs in (and out); F looks from outside.
    • It digs 8 m wide and 6.8 m high. Driving costs nothing - drilling eats energy three times as fast.
    • Empty, it goes on at half speed: refuel it at your base's MACHINE CHARGING STATION - the glowing signs beside the doorways; park anywhere near them.
    • No guns inside: the cutter and the tracks are its weapons. 8 rockets wreck one.

    Every kill hands you the next gun. Climb the whole ladder and win with the knife. Play it from the GUN GAME tile on the home screen, or alone in PRACTICE.

    🪜The ladder
    • Rocket launcher (2 kills) → railgun → pulse rifle → scattergun → pistol → knife.
    • A knife kill on the last step wins the round. 7 kills in all.
    • The bots climb the ladder too.
    🔪The knife
    • Everyone carries one all the time.
    • Get knifed and you lose a level.

    Counter-Strike 1.6 rules on de_dust2. PLAY 5v5 in the main menu puts you in a 5 v 5 match right away — empty places are filled with bots, and real players who join take a bot's place. SERVERS lists the 5v5 servers: pick one, and your side.

    💣How a round is won
    • Terrorists (orange, south): plant the bomb on A or B — or eliminate every CT.
    • Counter-terrorists (blue, north): defuse it — or eliminate every T before the plant — or hold out: time up = CTs win.
    • After the plant the bomb decides it: it explodes or it's defused.
    • Both teams wiped out at once: a draw.
    ⏱Match & rounds
    • One life per round — the dead watch a teammate.
    • 15 s freeze time (buy, no moving), then 1:45 to play.
    • 16 rounds (MR8): first to 9 wins; 8–8 is a draw.
    • After round 8 the teams switch sides (everyone back to $800).
    💣The bomb
    • One Terrorist carries it; if he dies it drops — any T picks it up.
    • Plant: hold E on a site for 3 s. G drops it for a teammate.
    • It explodes 35 s later (~20 m blast, both teams).
    • Defuse: hold E — 10 s, or 5 s with a kit.
    💰Money
    • Start $800 · max $16,000
    • Win: $3,250 (elimination / time) · $3,500 (bomb)
    • Loss: $1,400 +$500 per loss in a row (max $3,400); Ts +$800 if they planted
    • Plant / defuse +$300 · kill $300
    🛒Buy menu B
    • 1 Rifle $2,500 (T) / $3,100 (CT) · 2 Scattergun $1,700 · 3 Railgun $4,750
    • 4 Dynamite $300 · 5 Flashbang $200 · 6 Smoke $300
    • 7 Kevlar + helmet $1,000 · 8 Defuse kit $200
    • Everyone always has the Pistol.
    🎯Damage
    • Rifle: 5 body shots or 2 headshots. Pistol 22 (×2 head).
    • Railgun: a direct hit only — headshot kills, body 75, not through walls here.
    • No friendly fire (the bomb hurts everyone), no Overcharge.
    • Kevlar takes a third off everything but the rifle, railgun and bomb.
    🔫Guns on the ground · flashbangs
    • The dead drop their main gun — walk over it to take it; G drops yours first.
    • A flashbang blinds whoever looks at it 1.6 s after the throw — look away.
    ⌨Keys
    • B buy · E plant / defuse · G drop · 1 main · 2 pistol · 3 TNT · 4 flash · 5 smoke
    • Vote kick: Esc → 🗳 Vote kick, or /votekick name; F1 yes / F2 no.
    • Teammates have a ▼ over their heads; enemy names are red.

    • SKYREACH. Floating islands linked by indestructible bridges, massive jumps, updraft columns, sky cannons, waterfalls into the void — and THE LEVIATHAN, a colossal sky whale you can ride, with a deck, a shrine and an airstrike pickup. The GRAVITY STORM lowers gravity for 20 seconds.
    • IRONHOLD CASTLE. Mostly indoors: spired towers, gatehouse, keep, a coffered ballroom, the throne room. THE SIEGE — four trebuchets on the hills punch flaming boulders through the roof. When the candles blow out, THE GHOST KING walks the halls.
    • SANDSTORM. A walled, tactical lane map with two bomb sites. THE BOMB is planted every 150 s — stand on it to defuse, or watch the site go.
    • DUST, 1:1 — a faithful recreation built from the real navigation mesh — with COMPETITIVE 5v5: one life per round, buy time, plant and defuse, 16 rounds, first to 9, sides swap at half-time, party links for friends.
    • RAILGUN. A hitscan sniper laser that punches player-sized holes through every wall on the map.
    • Map styles and a decoration system; every map's buildings and walls are destructible.

    Improved

    • Anti-cheat. Every hit, blast and airstrike needs proof from the attacker's own broadcasts; the server bans only on proof (impossible damage, health or position) and never bans good players for being good.
    • Server-counted leaderboard. Kills and deaths are counted by the server from relayed hits, rate-capped — nothing a client claims counts on its own.
    • Nobody falls under a map any more: bedrock under the subway, solid stairs, pads lifted out of the terrain, and physics that ends every step above the ground.

    Fixed

    • A nuke or airstrike held over a round change gave a free second nuke. New rounds clear streaks; the server relays a nuke only after four server-proven kills.
    • Nuke kills no longer count towards the frag limit.
