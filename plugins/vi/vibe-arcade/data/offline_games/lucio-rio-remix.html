LÚCIO’S FIELD NOTES

RIDE THE RHYTHM.

Restore all 3 neighborhood stages and reach the finish before curfew. Switch to the Healing song inside each teal stage zone until it’s live.

↔

FIND YOUR LINE

A D / ← → skate left & right.
W / ↑ accelerate. S / ↓ brake.
Forward momentum is automatic.

↗

GET OFF THE GROUND

SPACE jumps. Jump at either neon wall, then hold toward it to ride. Jump again to kick off with bonus speed.

⇄

SWITCH THE ENERGY

SHIFT crossfades. Speed moves you faster. Healing restores vitality and stages. E amps your song for 3 seconds.

◎

MAKE SOME NOISE

F or click the route to blast nearby obstacles. Gates and wall rides charge Q Sound Barrier: 5 seconds of protection.

OUR CITY. OUR SOUND.

Lúcio Correia dos Santos turned music into a movement. This original fan minigame celebrates his joyful community activism, sonic technology, and refusal to let Rio fall silent.

The character and Overwatch belong to Blizzard Entertainment. World, game, character assembly and original procedural soundtrack were created for this experience.
