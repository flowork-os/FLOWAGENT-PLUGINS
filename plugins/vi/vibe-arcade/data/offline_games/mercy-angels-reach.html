VALKYRIE FIELD MANUAL

A little help.
A second chance.

Survive 75 seconds with at least three allies alive. Their rescue platforms are under drone attack. Your staff links automatically to the selected ally when you are close enough.

01

Read the signals.

Click an ally in the airspace or roster. Gold means linked. Red means critical. Tab cycles targets.

02

Close the distance.

WASD or arrow keys fly left, right, up and down on screen. Shift flies straight toward your selected ally.

03

Choose your beam.

1 heals. 2 boosts your ally's drone fire. Space swaps beams. Left/right click empty air also select heal/boost.

04

Make the save.

A downed signal lasts 14 seconds. Fly close and press E; stay still for 1.65 seconds to revive. Charge Q through support to link everyone with Valkyrie.

Leave red pulse rings before they detonate. Pause with Esc or P. On touch, use the flight pad and ability buttons.
