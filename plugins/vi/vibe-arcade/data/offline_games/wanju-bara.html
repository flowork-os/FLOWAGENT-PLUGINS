Traditional Punjabi Street Game

WANJUBARA

Lead nine runners past eight diving defenders
and their Captain. Get your team across!

DIFFICULTY

WASD / Arrow keys to run · Esc to pause

How to play

C

Your team of 9

A runner captain and 8 runners start in the blue strip. You control one (the yellow arrow); your teammates read the defence and pick their moments.

1–8

Defenders

Each guards one half of a lane. They watch your line, move to cut it off, and dive to tag you — feet stay on the line, but the dive reaches out.

C

The Captain

Runs the middle lane and every defender lane, and dives too. Keep an eye on the gold ring at the crossings.

Winning

A tagged runner is OUT and sits on the bench. Get 5 of 9 across before the clock runs out (6 on Hard). A missed dive leaves the defender on the ground — that's your gap!
