Carry the Ring

Take the One Ring from the Shire to Mount Doom on a living, 3D version of the map. Sneak past the Black Riders, hide in forests, wear the Ring (at a price) and discover 21 places along the way.

Controls: click/drag or WASD to walk · Space to wear the Ring · mouse wheel to zoom · P pause · M mute. Best with headphones.

Map art by Daniel Reeve. An unofficial, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with the Tolkien Estate or its licensees.
