    How to play

    Thrust is free. Speed costs heat.

    A space racer on a magnetic strip that loops round a dead star. There is no throttle: the strip carries your hull, and boost is the only acceleration. Boost, weapons and shields all cost heat, and you only shed it by radiating, so the sunlit half of the lap is where you cook and the eclipse is where you recover. Bots fly every hull nobody has; whoever arrives takes one over where it is flying. Every mesh, texture and sound effect is generated in code.

    • The strip carries your hull: there is no throttle to hold.
    • Boost is the only acceleration. It costs heat, and so do weapons and shields.
    • Brake into a corner with the stick pushed into it to slide through.
    • Fly the gates for ordnance, then fire it at the hulls ahead.
    • Three laps; then a new grid, rotated so nobody keeps pole.
