How to Play

  • A / D: Steer left or right.
  • W / S: Flip while airborne.
  • Space: Hop.
  • Shift: Use one boost charge.
  • P: Pause.
  • R: Restart.
  • Mobile: Use the on-screen stick to steer and flip. Tap Hop to jump and the lightning button to boost.
    Hit ramps at higher speeds for bigger jumps. Collect orbs and rings, perform flips, and skim past obstacles to increase your multiplier and refill boost. Avoid coral blocks and spinners—they end your run. Orange cones slow you down.

About

Race across a winding ribbon of road beneath a surreal sunset. Launch off ramps, perform midair flips, and chain rings, orbs, and near-misses to build your multiplier. Dodge obstacles, unleash your boost, and chase a new high score in this fast-paced motorcycle stunt runner.
