          How to play

          Steer

          Mouse, WASD / arrows, or the left thumbstick.

          Eat dew

          Glowing dew grows your jelly. Chain pickups fast for a combo multiplier (up to ×2).

          Trails burst rivals

          Everyone leaves a glowing trail. Touch another jelly's trail and you splat. Cut them off!

          Hop

          Click / Space / HOP to leap over trails — a ring shows where you'll land. Vaulting a trail fills your Glow.

          Stomp

          Land a hop on a much smaller jelly to burst it flat. A gold landing ring means a stomp is coming.

          Surge

          Hold Shift or right-clickHold SURGE to speed up. Costs size — you shed goo others can eat.

          Supernova

          Vaults, close calls, splats and combos fill Glow. At 100%: 7 seconds of speed, magnetism, trail immunity — and you swallow smaller jellies on touch.

          Currents

          Ride the blue tide streams the way their chevrons point for +45% speed. Against them you slow down.

          Power bubbles

          Magnet pulls dew, Shield survives one hit, Turbo gives free surging.

          Pond events

          Every few minutes a ring appears on the floor. The banner says what to do: catch the Golden Jelly, race to the falling star, hop into the piñata…

          Emote

          Keys 1–4The 💬 button pops a bubble over your jelly. Pick your four in the Locker.
