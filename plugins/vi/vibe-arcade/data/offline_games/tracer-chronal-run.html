London’s lights are running low. Recover three stolen chronal cells from rooftop security and bring them home.

  1. 01 / CRACK & COLLECTAim at an orange cell lock and hold the mouse button. When its shield breaks, run into the cell.
  2. 02 / KEEP MOVINGDrones mark their next shot with a red line. Change direction or Blink out of danger. Every cell raises the alarm.
  3. 03 / BRING IT HOMEAfter all three cells, return to the cyan HOME pad. Remaining time and health boost your final score.
