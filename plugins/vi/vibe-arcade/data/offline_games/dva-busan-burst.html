PLAY TO WIN.

Protect Busan through two drone waves, then defeat the Gwishin Leviathan. Keep your armor above zero.

WASD / ARROWSFly the harbor

Left is screen-left. Up flies toward the top. Your flight frame stays fixed as Tokki banks.

SPACE / LEFT MOUSEFusion Cannons

Hold to fire straight up the harbor. Line up with a drone to land your shots.

F / RIGHT MOUSEDefense Matrix

Hold to absorb bullets in the mint field ahead. It uses energy and pauses your cannons. Release to recharge.

EMicro Missiles

Six homing rockets. An 8-second recharge rewards a well-timed counterattack.

SHIFTBoosters

Dash in your current movement direction; with no direction, dash up. Briefly ignore damage. Recharges in 5 seconds.

QSelf-Destruct

At 100% charge, eject and launch Tokki. After 2 seconds: boom. Fly with your Light Gun until a fresh mech arrives.

Chain eliminations within 5 seconds for a score multiplier. Absorbed fire charges your ultimate faster. On mobile, use the direction pad, hold FIRE, and tap your ability cards.
