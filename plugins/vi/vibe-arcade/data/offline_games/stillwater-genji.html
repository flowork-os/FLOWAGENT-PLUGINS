THE WAY

A calm mind.
A precise hand.

Overcome three garden sentinels. Their patterns grow stronger; your discipline becomes the edge.

01 / SHURIKEN

Aim with the mouse. Hold CLICK or SPACE to throw three stars.

02 / DEFLECT

Press E or right-click as a shot arrives. The first 0.28 seconds return a powerful perfect counter.

03 / SWIFT STRIKE

SHIFT dashes in your movement direction, or toward your aim. Cut through danger. A defeated sentinel resets it.

04 / DRAGONBLADE

Hits and returned shots build harmony. At 100%, press Q, close the distance, and click to slash for six seconds.
