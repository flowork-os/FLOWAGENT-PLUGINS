Rules & HUD

Three touches per side. Your team may play the ball up to three times — usually bump → set → spike — and the third touch has to send it over. The dots under the scoreboard count them.

No double touches. You can't play the ball twice in a row — a teammate has to touch it in between. A block doesn't count as a touch.

Over the net, inside the antennas. The ball has to cross above the net and between the two striped antennas.

BumpA

Press early. An action is armed the moment you press it and the contact happens on its own at the sweet spot. Better timing, better touch.

Every rally scores. The ball lands in the other court: your point. It lands in yours: their point.

Faults. Send it out, into an antenna or under the net and the other team scores. A ball touching the line is in.

Win the rally, serve next. The ball icon on the scoreboard shows which team serves.

Sets. A set goes to 11 or 15 points (pick Length in the menu) and has to be won by two. Best of 3 needs two sets.

Rotate on a side-out. When your team wins the serve back, everyone moves one spot clockwise and the player reaching position 1 (right back) serves. The mini court at the top left shows your spot.

Back row (6v6). The three players at the back may not block, and spike only after jumping from behind the 3 m line. They pass, dig and set; the front row attacks at the net.

Every set starts from the starting line-up, and the teams take turns to serve first. On the beach the two players keep their sides and simply take turns to serve.

Landing ring — where the ball comes down. Yellow and pulsing: your ball, go! Blue: a teammate's. White: their side. Red ×: it's going out, let it go. The outer ring shrinks as the ball gets close.

Pink crosshair — your aim. Serves, spikes and balls you send over go there.
