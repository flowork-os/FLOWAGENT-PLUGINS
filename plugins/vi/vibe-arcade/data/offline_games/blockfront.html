FIELD NOTES

  • Health regenerates after a few seconds out of fire. The screen bleeds red as you take damage.
  • Headshots deal bonus damage and score. Damage falls off with range — know your weapon.
  • Enemies react to gunfire, take cover, peek and flank. Marksmen give away their position with a scope glint.
  • Explosions carve the voxel terrain. Crates and cloth break under sustained fire.
  • Hardpoint waves: stand in the zone with no hostiles inside to capture.
