FIELD BRIEF / NOCTURNE

Patience is
the weapon.

Clear 12 hostile sentries across three contracts. Work from the rooftops; finish before time or exposure runs out.

01

Find the opening

Move your mouse to aim. Use WASD or arrows to scan the skyline. On touch, drag the scene or use the arrows.

02

Make it precise

Space or right click to scope. Wait for 100% charge, aim for the head, then click. Hold Shift to steady. R reloads.

03

Leave no trace

F hook to a new perch and lower exposure. E reveals cloaked sentries for 7 seconds. Q slows pursuers with a Venom Mine.

Perfect charged headshot: 300 points. Body elimination: 100. Streaks, time, and a clean exit add bonuses. Esc pauses.
