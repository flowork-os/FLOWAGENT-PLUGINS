Color Quest: a free color-by-number pixel game for kids

Color Quest is a free color-by-number pixel game for kids ages 4 to 10, playable in any web browser with no ads, no sign-up and no timers.

Color Quest is a calm, free color-by-number game that runs in the browser. Kids pick a pixel picture, match each numbered square to its color, and watch a little garden world come to life. It works on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers, with no ads, no sign-up and no timers.

How to play

  1. Pick a pixel picture, or turn your own photo into a puzzle.
  2. Tap a color in the palette, then tap the squares with the same number.
  3. Color five squares in a row to earn power tools.
  4. Finish the picture, then print it or share a replay.

What you can do

  • Color dozens of pixel pictures: animals, food, flowers, space scenes and more.
  • Earn fun power tools (Line Blaster, Paint Brush, Paint Rain, Bomb) by coloring in a row.
  • Turn a photo into a puzzle. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Print a picture and color it on paper.
  • Share a replay of a finished picture with family.

Questions parents ask

Is it free? Yes, completely: no ads, no purchases, no account.

What age is it for? Around 4 to 10. There are no lives or lost progress, and wrong taps just get a gentle hint.

Does it work offline? Yes. Add it to your Home Screen and it keeps working without a connection.

Is there a calm mode? Yes. Effects follow your device's Reduce Motion setting and can be switched in the header; motion becomes gentle fades.

What languages? English, Spanish and Vietnamese.

Can I print the pictures? Yes, every picture has a free printable page.

Is my child's data collected? There are no accounts. Progress stays in your browser, and photos never leave your device.
