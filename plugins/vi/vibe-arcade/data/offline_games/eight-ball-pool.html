8 Ball Pool

Choose a game

  • Break, then pot your suit (solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15). The first ball you sink after the break sets your suit.
  • Pot a ball and you shoot again; miss and it's your opponent's turn.
  • Scratching, hitting the wrong ball first, or missing every cushion is a foul: your opponent gets ball in hand.
  • Clear your suit, then sink the 8 ball to win. Sink it early and you lose.
