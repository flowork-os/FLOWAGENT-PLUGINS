Flight & boost

ONE HAND TO FLYDrive. Fly. Land.

Show one open hand to calibrate. Move your palm left / right to steer, raise to ascend and lower to descend. Centre to hold altitude. Make a fist to slow down; hold to stop or hover. Keyboard debug: arrows steer and change altitude, B brakes.

COIN BOOST8 coins → 4 seconds

Collect 8 coins to trigger an automatic speed boost. Flight does not spend coins.

Boost time is capped at 6 seconds. Race start gives a 2.4-second launch boost; Turbo pickups add 3 seconds. Timers and jumps freeze when driving pauses.
