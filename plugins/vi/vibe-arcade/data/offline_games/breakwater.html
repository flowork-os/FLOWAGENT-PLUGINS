BREAKWATER: 3v3 ARAM MOBA

Creator age rating · 13+

A 3v3 ARAM MOBA you play in your browser, built for a phone held upright. One lane runs up your screen: a stone breakwater across the sea between two island keeps. Every hero is random, every fight is on the same narrow dyke, and a match lasts about eight minutes. Eight heroes: a chain-hooking tank, a whirlwind bear-cloak bruiser, a sun knight, a shadow assassin, a harpoon crossbow marksman, a fire mage with a meteor, a frost support and a drowned sorcerer whose whirlpool pulls whole teams together. The tide goes out every 90 seconds: sandbars rise from the sea with healing pearls and room to flank, then the water floods back and squeezes everyone onto the dyke. Minion waves, towers, a Tide Core to break, items that buy themselves, three bot difficulties. Touch joystick with tap-to-cast or drag-to-aim skills, or keyboard and mouse. Best match score wins the leaderboard.

A claymation bomber battle game: drop bombs, chain blasts and be the last clay critter standing. Classic grid bomb-arena action in your browser, made by hand-pressed plasticine. Artillery Moles: Turn-Based Team Artillery Battle

Artillery Moles is a turn-based team artillery game: command a squad of hard-hatted moles, read the wind, and blast the enemy team off a fully destructible hill of painted earth. Each turn one mole walks, jumps, picks a weapon from the weapon wheel and fires before the 45-second clock runs out — bazooka, grenades, cluster bombs, shotgun, dynamite, homing missile, crow strike, angry hedgehog, the Grand Marrow and more — then scurries to cover. Every crater is permanent, knockback hurls moles into the sea, and the grappling rope, bottle-rocket pack and burrowing drill get you anywhere. Play the Burrow League against ever-smarter CPU teams for a high score, or set up hot-seat matches for up to 4 teams on one device. Keyboard + mouse or touch.

Knock a horde leader down, step into the golden seal ring under its feet and hold your ground while the swarm closes in. Fill the seal and it joins you.
