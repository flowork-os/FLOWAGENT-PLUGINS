    THE FIELD

      How to fly

      A ←
      Bank left — the pod turns toward the side you press, always, in every camera.
      D →
      Bank right.
      W ↑
      Throttle up. Let go to ease off and coast.
      S ↓
      Air brakes. Also sheds engine heat faster.
      Shift Space
      Boost — huge thrust, builds heat. At 100% the engines overheat and choke.
      C
      Camera: chase · cockpit · high chase. Steering never changes.
      P Esc
      Pause. R restarts from the pause menu or results.
      M
      Mute / unmute.

      Glancing blows shear voxels off your engines. Hit something square at speed and you crash — pit droids will put you back on the line. Thread obstacles closely for near misses: they vent heat. Green gantries in the refinery patch engines on the fly. Throttle up just as the lights go green for a perfect launch — hold it too early and you flood the engines.

      Gamepad: left stick / d-pad steer, RT throttle, LT brake, X or RB boost, Y camera, Start pause. Touch: ◀ ▶ bank, THR / BRAKE / BOOST on the right (auto-throttle available in Settings).

      Settings

      There is no inverted steering mode: left is always left.
