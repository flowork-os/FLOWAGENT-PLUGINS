Pressure Wash Panic

chongdashu

Pressure Wash Panic is a juicy little cleaning game: sweep a thick, glossy jet across Maple Drive and watch crisp, clean stripes open up behind you, with splashes, flying gunk and satisfying pops as
each panel comes up spotless.

Switch between two nozzles.
The wide FAN is quick and gentle on dust; the narrow JET blasts through grime and oil stains, but it is also brutal on anything delicate.

The car, the tulips, the gnome, the windows and a sleeping cat all react when they get wet, and soaking one earns a strike.

Clean all seven panels before the three-minute clock runs out.
Finish under par for a speed bonus, chain panels for combos, and keep your record clean for
three stars.
Three strikes and you're fired.
