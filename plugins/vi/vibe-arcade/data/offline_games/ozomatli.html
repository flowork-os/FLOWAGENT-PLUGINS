Ozomatli

Leaderboard

Swing, climb and leap through the jungle as Ozomatli, a gibbon on a quest to open the Temple of Quetzalcoatl. A free 3D platformer in your browser. Find all eight glyphs hidden across the jungle to unlock the temple door. Swing across sacred cenotes on vines, climb walls and Atlantean columns, haul yourself up towers and solve the Hall of Pillars, where altar buttons turn the walkways. Throw cacao pods at enemies and restock at cacao bushes, and stay out of the water: gibbons hate it. Spin in mid-air to land 180s and 360s for bonus coins. Three levels of jungle, ruins and temple, with music throughout. Controls: keyboard and mouse, Xbox controller or touch.
