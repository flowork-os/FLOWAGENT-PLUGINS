# TradingAgents Sovereign Council

## Architecture Overview
The **TradingAgents Sovereign Council** operates as an institutional multi-agent investment committee designed to eliminate single-agent "yes-man" confirmation bias and grounded hallucinations:

1. **Analyst Team (Parallel Execution)**:
   - **Market Analyst**: Price action, OHLCV volume, moving averages, MACD, RSI, Bollinger Bands, and verified price level anchors.
   - **Sentiment Analyst**: Live zero-key StockTwits stream + Reddit RSS discussion posts (`wallstreetbets`, `stocks`, `investing`, `CryptoCurrency`).
   - **News & Macro Analyst**: Global macro headlines and Polymarket prediction market crowd-implied event odds.
   - **Fundamentals Analyst**: SEC EDGAR XBRL corporate filings (as-filed without restatement leakage) and market capital profile.

2. **Dialectic Debate Arena**:
   - **Bull Researcher**: Advocates upside potential, growth catalysts, and counters bearish objections.
   - **Bear Researcher**: Stress-tests downside risks, debt burdens, overvaluations, and market headwinds.
   - **Research Manager**: Impartial arbiter weighing debate evidence into a structured 5-tier recommendation (`Buy`, `Overweight`, `Hold`, `Underweight`, `Sell`).

3. **Tactical Execution & Risk Gate**:
   - **Trader**: Translates research plan into concrete execution levels (Entry, Stop-Loss, Position Sizing).
   - **Risk Committee**: Aggressive, Conservative, and Neutral analysts evaluate portfolio exposure.
   - **Portfolio Manager**: Final gatekeeper verifying cash allocation, current book context, and lessons learned from past settled trades.

4. **Sovereign Closed-Loop Learning**:
   - Tracks pending trades and automatically settles realized PnL and Alpha against regional benchmarks (`SPY`, `^N225`, `BTC-USD`).
   - Self-generates reflective lessons stored in sovereign memory.
